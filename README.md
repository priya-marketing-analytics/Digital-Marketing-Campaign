# Digital Marketing Campaign Analysis – Google Ads & Social Media

## Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end digital marketing campaign executed to promote
Wright State University’s **Master’s in Marketing Analytics & Insights (MSMAI)** program
to prospective students in **New Delhi, India**.

The campaign focused on **awareness, engagement, and conversions** using a
multi-platform strategy across **Google Ads, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube,
and influencer marketing**.

## Campaign Objective
- Increase awareness of the MSMAI program in an international market
- Drive engagement and conversions through paid digital channels
- Evaluate campaign performance using key digital marketing metrics

## Campaign Duration & Budget
- Duration: 8 weeks
- Total Budget Allocated: $1,500
- Total Spend: $921

## Channels & Tools Used
- Google Ads (Search, Display, Video)
- Facebook & Instagram Ads
- YouTube Video Campaigns
- Influencer Marketing
- Google Ads Manager & Meta Ads
- Excel for performance tracking

## Target Audience
- Location: New Delhi, India (including Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad)
- Age Group: 18–34
- Devices: Mobile-first audience

## Key Performance Metrics
- Impressions
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- Cost Per Click (CPC)
- Conversion Rate
- Cost Per Conversion
- Audience Demographics

## Key Results
### Google Search Campaign
- 25K impressions, 2.99K clicks
- CTR: 11.8%
- Conversions: 172
- Strong keyword performance for terms related to STEM, scholarships, and online courses

### Google Display Campaign
- 69.1K impressions, 5.75K clicks
- CTR: 8.33%
- Avg CPC: $0.05
- Conversions: 292
- Video content significantly improved cost per conversion

### Google Video Campaign
- 9.8K impressions, 969 views
- Conversions: 42
- Strong engagement on mobile devices

### Social Media & Influencer Marketing
- Facebook and Instagram generated the highest impressions
- Video storytelling drove stronger engagement than static posts
- Influencer collaborations increased reach and credibility

## Optimization & Strategy Evolution
- Budget reallocation based on campaign performance
- Prioritized high-performing Display and Video campaigns
- Improved CTR through keyword optimization and ad copy testing
- Shifted focus toward mobile-first content

## Business & Marketing Insights
- Storytelling through video significantly improves engagement
- Display ads offer strong cost efficiency for awareness campaigns
- Mobile devices dominate digital engagement
- Audience targeting (18–34 age group) is critical for education marketing
- Continuous monitoring and optimization improve ROI

## Deliverables
- Post-campaign performance report
- Channel-wise performance analysis
- Strategic recommendations for future campaigns
